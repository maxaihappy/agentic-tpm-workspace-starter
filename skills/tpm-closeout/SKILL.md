# TPM Closeout

## Workflow
1. Resolve one project and load approved acceptance criteria, inherited commitments, rollout and rollback obligations, operational handoff, receiving-team acceptance, remaining risks, and closure authority.
2. Assess every agreed criterion as `satisfied`, `missing`, `uncertain`, or `not applicable by explicit decision`.
3. Link each assessment to evidence with exact source identity, version or revision when available, and verification time.
4. Do not infer acceptance from a status label, completion percentage, silence, or deployment state. Do not waive missing criteria.
5. Identify criteria requiring a named human decision, acceptance, risk decision, or exception.
6. Prepare a closure report, retrospective, reusable lessons, and remaining-action list. Keep project facts separate from proposed reusable method changes.
7. Close records and stop follow-ups only when native platform and connector controls, source permissions, organizational policy, explicit task constraints, and reserved human decisions permit it. ChatGPT Project context cannot authorize closure. Read back closed state and verify affected reminders or schedules are actually stopped.
8. Preserve partial closeout and pending repairs. If evidence is unavailable, report uncertainty and continue with the rest of the assessment.

## Output
Provide a criterion-by-criterion table, inherited commitments, handoff and receiving-team acceptance, remaining risks, rollout/rollback obligations, decisions needed, closure recommendation, and verification state. Recommend closure only when required evidence and approvals are present.
