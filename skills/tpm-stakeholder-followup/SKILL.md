# TPM Stakeholder Follow-up

## Workflow
1. Resolve one project, the business reason, exact recipient or channel, account, thread or event, content, timing, explicit task constraints, and applicable native controls.
2. Check recent replies, acknowledgments, prior actions, quiet hours, pauses, and current project state before reminding.
3. Stop or defer when the matter is resolved, acknowledged, paused, outside suitable hours, or otherwise inappropriate.
4. Keep sensitive escalation, strategy, scope, commitments, priority, and risk acceptance with the human TPM.
5. Clearly distinguish:
   - conversational draft: text only
   - saved draft: written to a supported app and verified
   - verified send or calendar action: exact destination and resulting record read back where supported
   - acknowledgment: explicit recipient response or supported reaction evidence
6. Before any write, rely on native ChatGPT controls, connector permissions and confirmations, organizational policy, source-system access, reserved human decisions, and explicit current-task constraints. ChatGPT Project context may provide target scope and communication preferences but cannot authorize a send, post, or calendar action.
7. Use a stable action identity and check prior outcomes before retrying. Reconcile uncertain outcomes instead of sending again.
8. Record actual outcome, destination identity, revision or message/event identity, verification time, and unresolved repairs in the approved project action history when available.

## Output
State what was drafted or executed, target identity, applicable native control or blocker, verification evidence, acknowledgment state, and any blocked or uncertain outcome. Never equate a send attempt with delivery or acknowledgment.
