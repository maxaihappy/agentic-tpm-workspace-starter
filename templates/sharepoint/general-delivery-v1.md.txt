# General delivery runbook v1

Project ID: <PROJECT_ID>
Adapt this runbook to the project before live use.

1. Define purpose, scope, owner, intended outcomes and acceptance evidence.
2. Preserve approved baseline separately from forecasts and proposals.
3. Track deliverables and dependencies with stable IDs and confirmed owners.
4. Reconcile scoped evidence; distinguish a question, idea, action and decision.
5. Produce reports for the intended audience with explicit cutoff and coverage.
6. Coordinate requested follow-ups and retain verified outcomes.
7. Assess every exit criterion, receiving-team acceptance and residual obligations before recommending closure.

Project-specific lifecycle, risk thresholds, escalation triggers, retention, classification and acceptance rules must be supplied by the project owner. This generic runbook supplies no production operating authority.
